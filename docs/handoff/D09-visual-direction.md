## Selection rationale and alternatives

The owner prefers C's calm premium light appearance, light icon-led navigation, muted teal primary actions, deep ink typography, balanced density, soft neutral surfaces, moderate rounding/elevation and clear status/focus treatments. C combines the comfort sought in A with the workspace precision demonstrated by B. It is preferred over A for its more balanced structure and over B for its lighter, calmer navigation treatment; neither alternative is rejected as defective.

A and B remain historical alternatives, not active directions: A [desktop](https://www.figma.com/design/o5x1eCbKM7xPx7SKB0hsI9?node-id=76-25) / [Arabic](https://www.figma.com/design/o5x1eCbKM7xPx7SKB0hsI9?node-id=76-121) / [style](https://www.figma.com/design/o5x1eCbKM7xPx7SKB0hsI9?node-id=76-163); B [desktop](https://www.figma.com/design/o5x1eCbKM7xPx7SKB0hsI9?node-id=83-40) / [Arabic](https://www.figma.com/design/o5x1eCbKM7xPx7SKB0hsI9?node-id=83-169) / [style](https://www.figma.com/design/o5x1eCbKM7xPx7SKB0hsI9?node-id=83-197). ## Light, future Dark and bilingual boundaries

- Light preserves C's existing light sidebar and original icons. Do not create the previously proposed C-Navy sidebar variant.
- Future Dark Mode should have a distinct visual personality: explore navy/deep-ink navigation and darker workspace surfaces while retaining Taskly Orbit's identity and semantic/focus contrast. Do not simply invert Light colors. No Dark layout is designed or approved here; the existing small dark specimen is exploratory evidence only. Final colors and tokens are reserved for D10.
- English LTR and Arabic RTL are mandatory acceptance requirements in every subsequent design batch. Validate the changed UI in both languages, including Arabic-compatible typography, logical alignment/mirroring, wrapping, mixed IDs/dates and control placement. Fix material Arabic defects in the same batch. Preserve Inter/Cairo as current visual references; final typography values remain for D10.
